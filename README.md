# ATLIQ-Grands-
🏨AtliQ Grands - Revenue & Market Share Recovery | CRPC#1

Problem Statement :
AtliQ Grands, a 5-star hotel chain with 20+ years in hospitality, began losing market share & revenue due to rising competition and intuition-driven decisions. Leadership aimed to adopt Business & Data Intelligence to reverse this trend.

🎯 Project Objective :
To empower the Revenue & Management team with actionable insights by analyzing booking behavior, pricing, occupancy, cancellations, and channel performance enabling faster, data-driven decisions to reclaim market share.

Key Insights :
1️⃣Overall Performance
 - Weekdays drive ~70% of total revenue
 - ADR remained flat → no dynamic pricing
 - Weekend RevPAR higher despite lower volume
 - ₹299M revenue lost due to ~25% cancellations

2️⃣Rooms & Categories
 - Luxury segment contributes ~62% of revenue
 - Elite rooms show high demand but ~37% cancellations
 - Presidential rooms have highest ADR/RevPAR but low utilization usage
 - Standard rooms show lowest value perception

3️⃣City Performance
 - Mumbai leads in revenue, ADR & RevPAR
 - Delhi: highest occupancy but lowest revenue
 - Hyderabad underperforms on pricing metrics
 - Occupancy ≠ revenue → city-level pricing needed

4️⃣Bookings & Channels
 - Total bookings: 135K
 - 70% successful | 25% cancelled | 5% no-shows
 - OTAs contribute ~41% of bookings
 - Direct bookings only ~5% → low customer ownership

5️⃣Hotel Performance
 - Atliq Exotica, Atliq Palace & Atliq City lead revenue
 - AtliQ Blu: highest occupancy, lower ADR
 - AtliQ Seasons: lowest revenue (₹66M) & rating (2.3)

✅Recommendations :
  - Implement dynamic pricing based on demand, seasonality & events
  - Reduce cancellations via non-refundable rates & stricter policies
  - Boost direct bookings through loyalty programs & website optimization
  - Optimize weekend revenue with premium bundled packages
  - Improve low-performing hotels via service audits & targeted promotions
  - Reposition underperforming room classes to increase perceived value
  - Control OTA dependence by redefining channel strategy
